## Description (4000)
Flagstick Finder is the golf trip app for your crew — plan the trip, keep score together, and crown a winner.

PLAN THE TRIP
• Curated trip packages in 11 top golf destinations — Scottsdale, Myrtle Beach, Pinehurst, Kiawah & Charleston, Hilton Head, Naples, Orlando, Palm Springs, San Diego, Las Vegas and Northern Michigan
• 70+ hand-picked courses with photos, descriptions and links to each course's website and tee times
• Find trips near you — type your city or use your location to sort destinations by distance and drive time
• Book stays, flights, rental cars and tee times through trusted partners, plus restaurant picks and drive times
• Send the plan to your group chat and add the trip to your calendar in one tap

KEEP SCORE TOGETHER
• Fast hole-by-hole scoring for the whole group on one phone — big +/– buttons, swipe between holes
• Side games built in: Stroke Play, Skins, Stableford, Wolf, Best Ball, High-Low and Scramble
• Putts, fairways and greens in regulation for every player
• Live leaderboards and trip-long standings
• Share the results to your group chat as a clean scorecard image

THE CUP
• Split the group into two teams for a trip-long match-play cup
• A different format each day: Fourball, Foursomes (alternate shot), then Singles
• Points update as you score, with tee order for alternate shot and clinch alerts

SIDE BETS TALLY
• Log friendly side games and wagers and see exactly who owes whom
• Flagstick Finder never holds or moves money — it's a tally, you settle up yourselves

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Your trips and scores stay on your phone.
• No ads, no tracking.

Some booking links are affiliate links: booking through them costs you the same and helps keep Flagstick Finder free.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")
Answer: **Data Not Collected.**
Why this is accurate for 1.0: no accounts, no analytics/ads/tracking SDKs, no
crash reporting; trips/scores stay on the device; location is used only on
the device to sort trips and is never sent off it (Apple only counts data
"collected" if it's transmitted off the device). If crash reporting or
accounts are added later, this answer must be updated *before* that release.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")
No login is required — the app has no accounts in this version.

On first launch a short welcome tour appears. The app opens with an example trip ("Myrtle Beach Buddies Trip", under My Trips) so you can try the scorecard, The Cup and the side bets tally right away; it's labelled "Example" and can be deleted.

Suggested path: Explore → pick any trip package → "Start a trip from this package" → Book shows course websites / tee times and stay links (these open the partner's own site; some are affiliate links) → Scorecard → "+ New round" to score with +/– buttons → "Share results" sends a scorecard image via the share sheet.

Side Bets tally: a ledger of informal wagers between friends. The app does not process, hold, transfer or facilitate any payment or real-money gambling; it only adds up amounts the users type in.

Optional permissions, each requested only when the user taps the related button: Location (while using) for "Use my location" to sort trips by distance, computed on device; Calendar (write-only) for "Add to Calendar"; Photos (add-only) if the user chooses "Save Image" from the share sheet.
